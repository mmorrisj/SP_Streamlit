## 1. Key Findings (BLUF)

- **Oman balances multiple patrons** — Iran leads (35%) but engages U.S., China substantially too. **(M)**
- **Division of labor by instrument:** Economic→**China**, Social→**China**, Military→**Iran**, Diplomacy→**Iran**. **(H)**
- **Signature initiative:** Oman-China $200M Energy Transition Fund Launch for Vision 2040 (China). **(M)**
- **42.6% of U.S. coverage here is Iranian-media-framed** — a meaningful adversarial-narrative presence around the U.S. role. **(M)**
